# Karrot | Chat Team

### Team Background

Karrot’s Chat team connects users across the platform’s services, including secondhand trading, local jobs, real estate, and community groups. Chat is essential to the user journey, enabling users to coordinate transactions, discuss opportunities, and build connections.

As a **shared touchpoint** for services at different stages of growth, the team collaborates with service teams to address diverse needs while maintaining a consistent communication experience.
